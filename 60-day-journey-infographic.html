Applying AI to real workflows and starting to think like a builder — not just what AI can answer, but what it can help ship.

Understanding how AI systems maintain context, make decisions, and act autonomously — the conceptual groundwork for designing a modular, swappable AI engine.
Days 56–59 · Collaboration

Exploring how multiple AI capabilities coordinate toward a single goal — echoed directly in this capstone's multi-stage pipeline: parsing → extraction → interview → report.

Defend Your Experience

A 10-day, solo-built, fully deployed AI-powered interview simulator — every skill above converged into requirements gathering, system design, implementation, testing, deployment, and a public v1.0.0 release.
